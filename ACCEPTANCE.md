# Cradle acceptance

## Target contract — 16 October 2026, revised 17–19 October 2026

CONTRACT.md now describes the complete Airtable, Smartsheet and Quickbase replacement. Every clause's required acceptance evidence is listed below with the status in CONTRACT.md's current Status table. As updated on 19 October, the rows total **74 done, 108 partial, 28 missing** of 210 clauses, after the 17 October revisions that answered the first buyer review with twelve new clauses and the second with three (AUTO-USER-010, FIELD-005 and VIEW-017), each with sharper acceptance on existing ones. A row moves to done only when the named journey or test passes through a public door on the exact candidate and the clause's status in CONTRACT.md is updated in the same change. The earlier sections below remain binding records of what has been accepted and of the flagship gates.
