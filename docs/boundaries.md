# What Cradle is not

The sibling links below identify separate Run Edit Run products; they do not promise a Cradle integration or a sibling capability.

## Project and work management

Cradle is not a project or work management suite. Task inboxes, workload and
resource planning across projects sit outside Cradle. The separate product is
[Pegboard](https://run-pegboard.com).

## CRM

Cradle is not a CRM. The separate product is [Orbit](https://github.com/just-every/run-orbit).

## Surveys

Cradle is not a survey platform. The separate product is [Pencil](https://github.com/just-every/run-pencil).

## Wiki

Cradle is not a wiki. The separate product is [Slate](https://github.com/just-every/run-slate).

## File storage

Cradle is not file storage. The separate product is [Chest](https://github.com/just-every/run-chest).

## Team chat

Cradle is not team chat. The separate product is [Nook](https://github.com/just-every/run-nook).

## Issue tracking

Cradle is not an issue tracker. The separate product is [Vector](https://github.com/just-every/run-vector).

## Automation platform

Cradle is not an automation platform. The separate product is [Crank](https://github.com/just-every/run-crank).

## Identity provider

Cradle is not an identity provider. The separate product is [Auth](https://github.com/just-every/run-auth).

## Website builder

Cradle is not a website builder. The separate product is [Pane](https://github.com/just-every/run-pane).

## Regulated systems of record

Cradle is not an accounting ledger, payroll, EHR or payment processor. The separate payroll product is [Envelope](https://run-envelope.com). Money moves only through the buyer's processor (Stripe); ledgers stay in the buyer's accounting system. Cradle makes no HIPAA, PCI DSS or SOC 2 claim; a covered entity storing PHI needs its own hosting agreement (BAA) and its own assessment.
