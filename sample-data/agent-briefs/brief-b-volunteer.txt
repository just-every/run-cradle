# Brief B: a volunteer shift roster.
Base: Volunteer shift roster
Volunteers: Name (text, primary, required), Email (text), Phone (text)
Shifts: Date (date, required), Role (select: Kitchen | Front desk | Delivery | Cleanup), Slots (number)
Signups: Volunteer (link to Volunteers), Shift (link to Shifts), Confirmed (checkbox)
Views: a grid of each table; a calendar-ordered grid of Shifts by Date
Forms: a form for Signups titled "Sign up for a shift"
